## 3. Risks

### Team member is unavailable for a long time or leaves the team

**a. Probability:** Low. All team members are committed, and none of them intends to leave. Two members study abroad from February 2027 but keep working remotely, as registered with the course.

**b. Impact:** The loss of a team member would slow down the project. The most critical case would be the loss of the technically strongest member, since the others would have to acquire this member's knowledge.

**c. Mitigation:** Clear documentation, meeting minutes and clearly assigned tasks enable the remaining members to familiarise themselves with the work quickly and to redistribute the tasks. In the case of a long-term or permanent absence, we inform our course supervisor and notify the course organisers.

### Team member (repeatedly) does not keep agreements

**a. Probability:** Medium.

**b. Impact:** Other team members would have to take over the affected tasks, or milestones would be missed. This would delay the project and lead to point deductions.

**c. Mitigation:** If a team member does not respond on Discord, we contact them via WhatsApp and, if necessary, by phone. If there is no response within one to two days, their task is reassigned and the course supervisor is informed. If a member fails to meet an agreed deadline, a member with more available time takes over, and the member concerned compensates for this later with additional work. If this happens repeatedly, the team reserves the right to ask the course organisers, with justification and evidence, to adjust the distribution of points.

### Resources and their availability

**a. Probability:** Low. The required resources include a virtual machine, sample data in RDF format and the ontologies.

**b. Impact:** If one of these resources is not provided, the results would be less accurate and might not fully meet the client's expectations.

**c. Mitigation:** If the sample data in RDF format or the ontologies are not provided, we work with small example triples and a publicly available ontology until the client's data arrives. Without a virtual machine, we can still continue our work.

### Client is unavailable or does not keep agreements

**a. Probability:** Medium. The client has already supervised two TPSE teams, but delays in responses or deliveries can still occur.

**b. Impact:** The impact would be severe. The project would slow down or come to a standstill, milestones would be missed, and our work might go in the wrong direction.

**c. Mitigation:** We address problems with the client as early and openly as possible. If the client changes agreements afterwards, we clarify this with the course supervisor on the basis of our documentation and meeting minutes, which show what was agreed. If information is missing, we continue working with assumptions based on our best knowledge. Once the client responds, we discuss these assumptions with them. If Ning Xia cannot be reached before the next meeting and does not attend it either, we inform the course supervisor and subsequently the course organisers.
